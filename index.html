Our story

A corner shop with a roaster in the back

Tamarind Lane started as a single table and a two-kilo roaster. We buy green coffee from estates in Chikmagalur and Coorg, roast it a few days a week, and brew it the way we like it at home.

The chai comes from the other half of the family: a recipe that has been boiled on the same stove for three generations.

Come for a quick cup, or bring a book and take the corner seat.
